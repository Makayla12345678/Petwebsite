Pet Insurance

The right insurance plan can protect both your pet and your finances from unexpected costs.

Do You Need Pet Insurance?

A single emergency vet visit can cost hundreds or thousands of dollars. Pet insurance gives you the freedom to make decisions based on your pet's health rather than your bank balance. It's most valuable when taken out while your pet is young and healthy, before any pre-existing conditions develop.

📄

Types of Cover

Accident-only: covers injuries from accidents. Accident & illness: adds cover for illnesses and conditions. Comprehensive/wellness: also covers routine care like check-ups, vaccinations, and dental. Choose based on your budget and risk tolerance.

⚖️

What to Compare

When comparing policies look at: monthly premium, annual deductible, reimbursement percentage (typically 70–90%), annual or lifetime benefit limits, waiting periods, and exclusions. Pre-existing conditions are almost always excluded, so early enrolment is key.

💡

Tips for Saving

Insure young — premiums rise with age. Choose a higher deductible if you can cover small bills yourself. Some providers offer multi-pet discounts. Annual payment is usually cheaper than monthly. Always read the fine print on exclusions before signing up.

❓

Is It Worth It?

For most pet owners, yes. The peace of mind alone has value. Statistically, one in three pets needs unexpected veterinary care each year. A good accident & illness policy typically pays for itself after a single significant illness or injury.
