Veterinary Care

Your pet deserves quality healthcare. Here's how to find the right vet and keep costs manageable.

Why Regular Vet Visits Matter

Routine veterinary care keeps your pet healthy and catches problems early — when they're far easier and cheaper to treat. Annual check-ups, dental care, vaccinations, and parasite prevention are the foundation of a long, healthy life for your companion.

Don't wait until your pet shows signs of illness. Preventive care is the most cost-effective approach in the long run.

🔍

Choosing a Vet

Look for a vet who communicates clearly, takes time to answer questions, and makes your pet feel comfortable. Ask for recommendations from your shelter or neighbours. Visit the clinic before registering — a good practice will be happy to show you around.

💰

Managing Costs

Ask your vet about payment plans, wellness packages, or low-cost clinic days. Many areas have non-profit vet clinics offering reduced rates. Pet insurance can significantly reduce unexpected bills — see our Insurance page for guidance.

📅

Essential Appointments

First visit within a week of adoption, annual wellness check, dental cleaning every 1–3 years, vaccinations on schedule, and a vet visit whenever you notice changes in behaviour, appetite, or energy. Senior pets (7+) benefit from bi-annual check-ups.

🚨

Emergency Care

Know your nearest 24-hour emergency vet before you need it. Signs requiring immediate attention: difficulty breathing, collapse, seizures, suspected poisoning, severe vomiting or diarrhoea, eye injuries, or any trauma. When in doubt, call your vet.
