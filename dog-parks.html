Frequently asked questions

How many of these dog parks are off-leash and fenced?

All 10 parks in our directory are off-leash areas, and 4 are fenced, including Colonel Samuel Smith Park, Dufferin Grove Park and Greenwood Off-Leash Area. Use the Fenced and Off-Leash filters above to narrow the list.

What should I know before visiting an off-leash dog park?

Check the posted rules at the park, keep your dog's vaccinations up to date, and bring water, since not every park has a water source. Our legislation page covers licensing and leash rules in the GTA.
