Frequently asked questions

Are there free dog events in the GTA?

Yes. 6 of the 9 events in our directory have free entry, including Pampered Pooches T.O., Bark in the Park TO and Barket Market: Yonge + St. Clair Edition. Use the Entry filter above to show only free events.

When are dog events held in the GTA?

The events we list run in these months, with the number of events in brackets: August (5), November (2), September (1), October (1). Dates change each year, so check the event website before you go.
