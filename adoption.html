Adopt a Pet

Everything you need to know about finding and welcoming a new animal companion into your home.

Why Adopt?

Adopting a pet from a shelter saves a life. Millions of cats, dogs, and small animals enter shelters each year — giving one a home makes a profound difference. Shelter animals are often already vaccinated, microchipped, and assessed for temperament, making the transition into your home smoother.

Beyond the individual impact, adoption reduces demand for commercially bred animals and supports the vital work of local shelters and rescue organisations.

📋

The Adoption Process

Most shelters follow a straightforward process: browse available animals online or in person, submit an application, attend a meet-and-greet, and complete an adoption agreement. Some shelters conduct a home visit. The process typically takes a few days to a week.

✅

Your Checklist

Before bringing your pet home: prepare a sleeping area, stock up on food and water bowls, buy a collar and ID tag, schedule a vet appointment within the first week, pet-proof your home, and plan for the adjustment period — new animals need time to settle in.

🐾

Choosing the Right Pet

Consider your living situation, activity level, and lifestyle. A high-energy dog needs space and regular exercise; a cat can thrive in a smaller home. Ask shelter staff for guidance — they know each animal's personality and needs well.

💛

The First Month

Give your new pet time to adjust. Follow the 3-3-3 rule: 3 days to decompress, 3 weeks to learn your routine, 3 months to feel truly at home. Be patient, keep the environment calm, and build trust with consistent care and gentle interaction.
